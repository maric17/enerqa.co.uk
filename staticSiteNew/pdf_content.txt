Services
Enerqa Consultancy provides specialized services across four key 
domains: Climate Change, Energy, Environment, and Business 
Solutions. In the Climate Change domain, Enerqa supports 
governments, corporations, and institutions in addressing climate 
challenges through greenhouse gas (GHG) inventories, carbon 
footprint assessments, climate risk analysis, and the development 
of climate adaptation and mitigation strategies. The company 
helps clients align with global climate commitments, such as the 
Paris Agreement and Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs), 
by enhancing Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) 
systems and integrating climate action into policy frameworks. 
Within the Energy sector, Enerqa provides consultancy services 
that optimize energy efficiency, promote renewable energy 
adoption, and support energy transition strategies. The company 
specializes in feasibility studies for solar, wind, and other 
renewable energy projects, as well as energy audits and grid 
integration solutions. Through advanced modeling and strategic 
planning, Enerqa assists stakeholders in reducing energy costs, 
enhancing sustainability, and ensuring compliance with 
international energy regulations. 
In the Environment field, Enerqa delivers environmental impact assessments (EIA), sustainability reporting, and ecological footprint analysis. The consultancy aids 
organizations in improving environmental performance, adopting circular economy practices, and ensuring regulatory compliance. With expertise in waste 
management, water conservation, and biodiversity protection, Enerqa contributes to the development of policies and frameworks that enhance environmental 
sustainability at national and corporate levels. For Business Solutions, Enerqa offers strategic advisory services that help organizations integrate Environmental, Social, 
and Governance (ESG) principles into their operations. The company provides ESG gap assessments, corporate sustainability planning, and green finance advisory. 
Through tailored consultancy, Enerqa enables businesses to align with global sustainability standards, enhance resilience, and leverage climate finance opportunities 
for long-term growth.

Enerqa Consultancy leverages a suite of proprietary and globally recognized tools to deliver 
impactful climate change solutions. Our comprehensive approach supports clients in

cutting-edge technology with deep methodological expertise to drive effective and sustainable 
outcomes.
Our Arsenal for a Sustainable Future:
We utilize a powerful combination of internally developed tools and established international 
methodologies, ensuring accuracy, transparency, and alignment with best practices.

Governance performance.

• ESIA Risk Assessment Tool: Identifying and mitigating environmental and social impacts.
• Green Project Scoring Tool: Determining eligibility for "Green Finance" initiatives.

This integrated approach empowers Enerqa to provide tailored solutions that meet our clients' 
specific needs and contribute to a cleaner, more sustainable future.
Imagine a small business striving to reduce its carbon footprint but struggling to 
track emissions efficiently. Or a large corporation needing to align with ESG 
regulations and investor expectations while managing complex supply chains. 
Perhaps a government entity is aiming to implement national sustainability policies, 
or an NGO wants to measure and report the impact of its climate projects with 
credibility. No matter the sector, the challenges are similar: where to start, how to 
measure impact, and how to ensure real progress.
At Enerqa, we understand that every organization is at a different stage in its 
sustainability journey—which is why our suite of customizable sustainability tools is 
designed to adapt to your unique needs, expertise level, and operational scale.
For businesses and corporates, our ESG Readiness Tool provides a clear roadmap to 
ESG excellence, helping companies identify gaps, track progress, and align with 
global sustainability standards. If you're a government body or policymaker, our 
GHG Emissions Calculator offers a precise, scalable solution for national carbon 
accounting and reporting. NGOs and project developers can rely on our MRV Tool for 
transparent monitoring and verification, ensuring their sustainability efforts meet 
funding requirements and deliver real impact. And for investors or institutions 
seeking green finance, our Green Project Scoring Tool simplifies the process of 
assessing project eligibility, making it easier to access climate funding and drive 
sustainable investments.
But what makes these tools truly powerful is how they work together. A corporate 
using our GHG Emissions Calculator to track its carbon footprint can seamlessly 
integrate the MRV Tool for compliance reporting. An SME looking to improve ESG 
performance can pair our ESG Readiness Tool with the Green Project Scoring Tool to 
unlock sustainability-linked financing opportunities. A government planning a

and social safeguards before launching a national initiative.
No matter your sector, expertise level, or sustainability goals, Enerqa’s tools are 
designed to simplify the process, eliminate guesswork, and provide actionable 
insights that drive measurable results. You don’t need to be a sustainability expert to 
get started—we provide the technology, guidance, and adaptability to make 
sustainability work for you.
The future of sustainability is here—customized, integrated, and built to help you 
succeed. Let Enerqa’s smart tools power your sustainability journey.
