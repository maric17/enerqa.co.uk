## Privacy Notice

### Enquiries

The contact form collects your name, email address, message and enquiry type. You may also supply your organisation, a domain or industry, project location, project stage or requested tool. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and manage prospective business relationships. Our lawful basis for general and business enquiries is legitimate interests in responding to requests and managing those relationships. Where you personally ask us to take steps towards a contract with you, the contractual or pre-contractual basis may apply instead.

Our enquiry-retention policy is two years after our last substantive contact. Routine editor changes do not restart that period. If an enquiry becomes a client engagement, relevant records follow a separate client-record retention policy. Any legally necessary exception must be documented and reviewed. Automated cleanup is not yet active; the retention policy must also be applied to relevant mailboxes, exports and backups. You can contact us about the information we hold and request deletion where applicable.

### Newsletter

Newsletter subscriptions require separate, optional consent through an unticked checkbox. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you unless you choose this option. You can withdraw consent using the [unsubscribe form](/newsletter/unsubscribe) or by emailing [info@enerqa.co.uk](mailto:info@enerqa.co.uk).

We retain subscriptions until you unsubscribe or the newsletter ends. After withdrawal, we keep only the minimum suppression record needed to respect your choice; any separate consent evidence must have a documented retention period. SendPulse is the chosen newsletter provider, but its delivery connection is inactive. The current site saves subscription requests in Enerqa's database; a saved request does not itself mean an email has been sent.

### Hosting, storage and external content

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Vercel Blob for uploaded files and Supabase for the content database. Hosting providers process technical connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the website. Authorised staff can access form submissions in the content editor. Enquiry notifications are intended for info@enerqa.co.uk and contain a protected link to the saved enquiry rather than a copy of its contents; provider delivery is currently inactive.

Some tools and data views are hosted by external services, including Gapminder. They load only after you choose to load an item or allow embedded content. Those services may receive connection information and use their own storage under their own policies. You can manage automatic loading on [Cookie Choices](/cookie-choices). Contact Privacy Contact — Enerqa for questions about hosting, processing locations, transfers or retention arrangements. We do not sell your personal information.

### Website analytics

Optional website analytics is not active. Enerqa has chosen self-hosted Umami for future measurements of page visits, dataset downloads and tool access, subject to a separate positive choice on [Cookie Choices](/cookie-choices).

The planned measurements exclude form contents, email addresses, search questions, URL queries and fragments, and contact, search, newsletter and administration routes. Session replay, advertising tracking and user-identification features are not enabled. The agreed retention limit is 90 days for raw events and associated sessions, including matching backup limits. Analytics will remain inactive until its deployment, consent and deletion checks are complete.

### AI search

AI search is disabled. No search questions are sent to an AI provider by this feature. We will update this notice before activating AI processing.

## Cookie Choices

### Essential storage

Essential storage supports the content editor, remembers your choices and remembers your language choice. A staff-authentication cookie keeps authorised staff signed in. Browser storage holds your independent embed and analytics choices and your language preference.

### Embedded third-party content

Optional embedded third-party content and optional website analytics have separate controls below. Both start switched off unless you have previously saved a choice.

When you allow embedded content, the selected external service may receive connection information and set its own cookies. You can load one embed for the current visit or allow embeds automatically. Turning automatic loading off does not withdraw a separate one-time choice for an already loaded embed; reload that page to close it. External services' own storage and policies may still apply to information already received.

### Optional website analytics

Umami analytics is planned but is currently inactive. Allowing analytics here does not activate the service while its release setting is off. When activated, it will load only after your separate positive analytics choice. Switching that choice off stops subsequent measurements; measurements already sent remain subject to the agreed deletion period.

The planned measurements exclude query strings, fragments, form contents, email addresses and AI search questions. No session replay, advertising tracking or user-identification feature is enabled. The agreed raw-event and session retention period is 90 days, including matching backup limits, and must be verified before activation.

### Changing your choices

Use the controls below to change either choice at any time. A saved change to one choice preserves the other. If your browser blocks storage, choices cannot be remembered and optional content will not load automatically. For more information, read the [Privacy Notice](/privacy) or email [info@enerqa.co.uk](mailto:info@enerqa.co.uk).

## Accessibility Statement

### Our accessibility target

Enerqa aims to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) Level AA. We do not claim full conformance without supporting testing of the whole site.

### What has been checked

Recorded tests cover keyboard navigation and form controls, the search dialog and mobile menu, visible focus, reduced-motion behaviour, consent-gated embedded content and several mobile data views. The search dialog and mobile menu retain keyboard focus while open, close with Escape and return focus to their trigger. The homepage provides a pause control for moving cards. Data Portal numerical views provide table alternatives to charts.

### Known limitations and further testing

The full page audit, contrast review, screen-reader checks, third-party embed review and responsive test matrix are not complete. Some pages may skip a heading level and some form focus colours still need review. External tools and embeds may have accessibility limitations outside Enerqa's control; use an available external-service link or contact us if a view is difficult to use. Approved Arabic equivalents are not yet available; the language control explains when a page remains in English.

This statement describes the available evidence and remaining limits. We will update it as testing and improvements continue.

### Report an accessibility problem

Email [info@enerqa.co.uk](mailto:info@enerqa.co.uk) or use the [contact form](/contact). Include the page address, the problem and any assistive technology you use so we can investigate and discuss an accessible alternative.
