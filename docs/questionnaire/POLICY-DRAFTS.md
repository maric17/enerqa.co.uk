## Privacy Notice — replacement sections

### Who is responsible for your information

Enerqa Ltd, company number 14034864, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Its registered address is College House, 17 King Edwards Road, Ruislip, London, HA4 7AE. For privacy enquiries, contact **Privacy Contact — Enerqa** at info@enerqa.co.uk.

### Enquiries

The contact form collects your name, email address, message and enquiry type. You may also supply your organisation, a domain or industry, project location, project stage or requested tool. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and manage prospective business relationships. Our lawful basis for general and business enquiries is legitimate interests, subject to a documented legitimate-interests assessment. Where you personally ask us to take steps towards a contract with you, the contractual or pre-contractual basis may apply instead. The final lawful-basis wording remains subject to legal review.

We keep contact-form enquiries for up to two years after our last substantive contact, then delete or anonymise them. If an enquiry becomes a client engagement, relevant records follow our separate client-record retention policy. Any legally necessary exception must be documented and reviewed. Routine editor changes do not restart the two-year period.

### Newsletter

Newsletter subscriptions require separate, optional consent through an unticked checkbox. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you unless you choose this option. You can withdraw consent at /newsletter/unsubscribe or by contacting info@enerqa.co.uk.

We keep subscriptions until you unsubscribe or the newsletter ends. We retain only the minimum suppression record needed to respect an unsubscribe request. Any necessary retention of separate consent evidence must be documented. SendPulse is the chosen newsletter provider. Its credentials and mailing-list configuration are not yet verified, so the current site saves subscription requests locally.

**After the live SendPulse connection is verified:** replace the last sentence with “We share your email address with SendPulse to manage the newsletter.” Confirm the actual account region, processor agreement and international-transfer arrangements before publication.

### Hosting and service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Vercel Blob for uploaded files and Supabase for the content database. Staff can access form submissions in the content editor. Notifications are intended for info@enerqa.co.uk and contain a link to the saved enquiry, rather than a copy of its contents. Confirm the actual hosting regions, processor terms, access permissions and backup-retention periods before final approval.

### Website analytics

Optional analytics is not active yet. Enerqa has chosen the free, open-source, self-hosted version of Umami. It will measure page visits, popular pages, dataset downloads and tool access only after you opt in on /cookie-choices. It will not use session replay, advertising tracking or individual user identification. Form contents, email addresses, search questions and personal information in URL parameters or fragments are excluded. Contact, search, newsletter and administration routes are excluded from measurement. You can withdraw your choice on /cookie-choices at any time.

**Retention period approved by the user on 3 October 2026:** delete raw analytics events and associated sessions after 90 days, with a daily purge and matching backup limits. The approved 90-day target is not evidence that deletion is already running. Do not activate analytics until the retention process is installed and verified. Self-hosting software can be free while infrastructure still has running costs.

Do not rely on a cookie-free or statistical-purpose exemption without a separate review of the actual implementation. Prior opt-in remains the default. Any approved UK statistical-purpose exception would also require clear information, a simple free opt-out, and appropriate aggregation and minimisation.

## Cookie Choices — replacement optional-services section

Essential storage supports the content editor, remembers your cookie choices and remembers your language choice. Optional embedded third-party content and optional website analytics have separate controls below. Both start switched off.

When you allow embedded content, the selected external service may receive connection information and set its own cookies. You can load one embed for the current visit or allow embeds automatically. Turning the automatic choice off does not withdraw any one-time choice you made for an already loaded embed; reload that page to close it.

Umami analytics is planned but is not active until its deployment, retention and policy checks are complete. When active, it loads only after a separate positive analytics choice. Switching analytics off stops subsequent measurements. Measurements already sent remain subject to the approved deletion period. Query strings, fragments, form contents and AI search questions are excluded. No session replay, advertising tracking or user identification is enabled.

Cookie-free software does not automatically remove consent requirements. This site uses prior opt-in unless an applicable exemption has been reviewed and explicitly approved.

## Accessibility Statement — confirmed target and evidence wording

Enerqa aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. We do not claim full conformance without supporting testing.

Our work includes keyboard navigation, labelled forms, visible focus, suitable colour contrast, controllable animations and reduced-motion support. Numerical data views should provide accessible table or text alternatives. We must also check maps and third-party embeds and describe any remaining limitations.

Recorded tests cover keyboard and form controls, consent-gated embeds, reduced motion and several mobile data views. The full page audit, contrast review, third-party embed review and test matrix remain open in HANDOFF-TASKS.md section 14.3. Do not describe these pending checks as completed. For an accessibility problem, email info@enerqa.co.uk or use /contact.
