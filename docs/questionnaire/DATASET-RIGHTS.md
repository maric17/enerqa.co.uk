# OWID and Ember commercial-use review — questionnaire Q20

Checked against primary sources on 3 October 2026. This is a source-rights review, not automatic approval to publish every provider dataset.

| Source | Finding | Current publication action |
|---|---|---|
| Ember | Its official licensing page releases Ember content under CC BY 4.0. That licence permits commercial sharing/adaptation with attribution, a licence link and disclosure of changes. Separate logo permission is required. | Commercial use is supported by the stated licence. Obtain the provider/content approval, verify the exact electricity CSV, variables, units, period and release, record evidence and attribution, then approve the CMS row. |
| Our World in Data | Its own data is generally CC BY, but much of its data comes from third parties whose own licences govern reuse. Obtain its exact metadata and underlying Global Carbon Budget release/licence and anonymous download evidence before publication. |

Sources: [Ember licensing](https://ember-energy.org/creative-commons/), [CC BY 4.0 commercial permissions and conditions](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [OWID reuse guidance](https://ourworldindata.org/faqs#can-i-reuse-or-republish-your-data), [current CO2 chart](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/co2-by-source).

The current seed names Global Carbon Budget v2023, but this is not evidence that the current chart/download uses that release. A direct request to the chart metadata endpoint failed during this pass; no replacement release, units or access-check date was invented. Keep both rows draft/unknown until their specific source/download evidence and provider approval are recorded. The Ember “renewable capacity” label must also be checked against the actual file: do not imply capacity values if the chosen series contains generation only.

For Ember attribution, include the exact dataset title and release, “Ember”, its source URL, CC BY 4.0 link and a note describing transformations. For OWID, credit both OWID and the underlying provider with the actual release/version and required licence. Enerqa’s own chart/table can be used once these data rights are confirmed; the OWID Grapher software has separate terms and is not cleared by the data licence.

The questionnaire asked to check commercial rights and report back. It did not itself approve these providers or remove the existing per-dataset publication/access gates. If approval or exact evidence cannot be obtained, replace the entries with appropriate approved-provider datasets rather than silently relabelling them.
