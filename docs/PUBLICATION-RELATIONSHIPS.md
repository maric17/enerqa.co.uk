## Reasons

- **The Evolution of Climate Change Understanding and Response:** Climate science history and international climate agreements are relevant to public policy and education.
- **Climate Forcers: The Hidden Drivers of Global Warming:** Long- and short-lived climate forcers from fossil fuels, industrial gases and agricultural emissions; country emissions provide related context.
- **Weathering the Storm: Climate Resilience in Supply Chains:** Supply-chain emissions and climate disruption affect transport, manufacturing facilities and ports; emissions inventories provide supporting context.
- **Climate Change and War: A Complex Interplay:** Climate security, environmental governance, displacement and climate-resilient recovery are public-policy priorities. No available tool or dataset specifically covers the conflict analysis.
- **Greenhouse Gases and Climate Change: An Overview:** Greenhouse gases, fossil-fuel and industrial emissions, biodiversity impacts and Paris Agreement policy; country greenhouse-gas data is directly relevant.
- **Breathing vs. Burning: The Carbon Footprint Contrast:** Compares the biological carbon cycle with fossil-fuel combustion and its emissions footprint; energy-sector and country emissions context fits.
- **GHG EMISSIONS – The Burden on Our Planet:** Overview of the greenhouse-gas burden and the need for emissions management, relevant to climate policy and country emissions inventories.
- **Exploring the Rainbow of Hydrogen Technology: A Path to Sustainable Energy and Climate Resilience:** Hydrogen production pathways, natural-gas feedstocks and decarbonisation of heavy industry and transport. No published hydrogen-specific tool or dataset is available.
- **Green Credit Lines in the Gulf Cooperation Council: Evaluating Opportunities, Overcoming Challenges, and Assessing the Impact on Energy Transition in:** Green credit lines, financial institutions and national policy for renewable investment and mitigation; country emissions inform the mitigation context. The green-project scoring tool is unvalidated and is not linked.
- **Supercritical Water Technology (SCWT) - A Sustainable Solution for Waste Management in Qatar:** Supercritical water waste treatment, wastewater reuse, industrial waste, nutrient recovery and energy recovery; municipal waste statistics relate to the waste-management context.
- **Driving Climate Action Through Renewable Energy Finance: Insights from an Expert:** Renewable-project financing, investment returns and public-private finance; easySOLAR supports initial solar-project cost and financial-performance assessment.
- **I-RECs - A catalyst for renewable energy investment in Qatar:** Solar PV deployment, renewable-electricity certificates, investment and emissions targets in Qatar; solar-resource data and easySOLAR support solar assessment, with country emissions providing context.
- **The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Environmental Sustainability:** AI applications in climate monitoring, efficient energy systems and water/waste management; emissions and waste datasets are related evidence. No published AI-specific tool is available.
- **The Hidden Costs of Your Burger and Pizza: What’s Really at Stake?:** Food-production externalities, environmental costs, carbon taxes and resource depletion; adjusted net savings captures pollution/resource costs at country level, and emissions data adds context.
- **Practical Tips for Reducing Food Waste at Home: A Step Towards Sustainability:** Domestic food waste, methane, municipal-waste generation and circular consumption; municipal-waste data is a direct subject match.
- **Sustainable Tourism:** Responsible tourism, ecosystem protection, cultural heritage and benefits for local communities. No available tool or dataset specifically covers tourism impacts.
- **The Hidden Link Between Cigarette Smoking and Climate Change:** The tobacco lifecycle connects farming and deforestation, manufacturing emissions, litter and public-health education. No tobacco-specific tool or dataset is available.
- **Empowering Communities Through Social Sustainability: A Call to Action:** Social inclusion, equality, migration, public services, health and education are the social pillar of sustainability. No direct matching tool or dataset is available.
- **The Origins of Urban Greening:** Urban green roofs, adaptive infrastructure, city planning, cooling and resilience; GreenScale Pro directly supports sustainability and resilience assessment in buildings and infrastructure.
- **Environmental Impacts of Mercury Use in Artisanal Gold Mining in Africa (Sudan- case): The Amplifying Effect of Torrential Rains and Floods:** Artisanal mining, mercury pollution, flood-driven contamination and public-health hazards. The ESIA tool is unvalidated, and no published mercury-pollution dataset is available.
- **Understanding the DPSIR, DPCER, DPSWR, and DPSER Frameworks in analyzing the interactions between human activities and the environment:** Environmental assessment frameworks support policymakers and researchers evaluating pressures, state, impacts and responses. No published framework-specific tool or dataset is available.
- **DPSIR Framework for Sustainable Aquaculture in the Red Sea Region (KSA): A Climate Adaptation and Mitigation Perspective:** Aquaculture, marine ecosystems, climate adaptation and mitigation, policy responses and explicit ESG integration recommendations; the ESG Readiness Tool supports that ESG assessment. No published aquaculture-specific dataset is available.
- **The Imperative for ESG Readiness Tools: Unlocking Benefits for Companies, Institutions, and Society:** Corporate ESG assessment, investor expectations, reporting and regulation; the article explicitly discusses Enerqa’s ESG Readiness Tool. No dataset covers the full ESG-readiness assessment.
- **Scope 4 Emissions: The Concept of Avoided Emissions:** Avoided emissions from renewable power, low-carbon products, transport and recycling; easySOLAR estimates avoided solar-project emissions and country inventories provide emissions context.
- **Sudan's Energy Balance 2020:** Sudan energy supply, petroleum imports/refining, electricity transformation and national energy-policy planning. No published dataset reproduces the 2020 energy balance, and no tool directly matches it.
